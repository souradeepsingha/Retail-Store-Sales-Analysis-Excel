# Retail Store Sales Analysis – Excel

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is a **Retail Store Sales Analysis Dashboard created using Microsoft Excel**.

The project analyzes retail order data to understand sales performance, customer demographics, order status, sales channels, age groups, and state-wise sales performance.

An interactive dashboard was created using **Excel Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers** to present the analysis in a simple and easy-to-understand format.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze monthly sales and order trends.
- Compare sales between men and women.
- Analyze order status distribution.
- Identify the top 5 states based on sales.
- Analyze orders by age group and gender.
- Analyze orders across different sales channels.
- Create an interactive Excel dashboard.
- Generate useful business insights from retail data.

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The Excel dashboard contains the following visualizations:

### 1. Orders vs Sales

A combination chart showing monthly:

- Sales Amount
- Number of Orders

This helps compare sales performance with order volume.

### 2. Sales: Men vs Women

A pie chart showing the sales contribution from male and female customers.

### 3. Order Status

A pie chart showing the distribution of orders by:

- Delivered
- Cancelled
- Refunded
- Returned

### 4. Sales: Top 5 States

A bar chart showing the top 5 states based on total sales amount.

### 5. Orders: Age vs Gender

A column chart comparing orders across different age groups and genders.

### 6. Orders: Channels

A pie chart showing the distribution of orders across different sales channels, including:

- Amazon
- Myntra
- Flipkart
- Ajio
- Meesho
- Nalli

## 🔍 Business Questions

The dashboard helps answer questions such as:

1. How do sales and order volume change by month?
2. Which gender contributes more to sales?
3. What is the distribution of order statuses?
4. Which are the top 5 states by sales?
5. Which age groups generate the most orders?
6. How do orders differ between men and women across age groups?
7. Which sales channels generate the most orders?
8. How does sales performance change by category?
9. How do sales and orders change when using the dashboard filters?

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- Women contribute a larger share of sales than men.
- Delivered orders represent the majority of orders.
- Amazon accounts for a significant share of orders among the available channels.
- Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Telangana, and Tamil Nadu are among the top states by sales.
- Order contribution varies across different age groups and genders.
- Monthly sales and order trends provide an overview of changes in retail performance.
